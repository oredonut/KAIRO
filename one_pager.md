# KAIRO: One-Pager — Squad Hackathon 3.0 🚀🛡️

## **Mission**
To empower the informal economy with an AI-powered economic identity that unlocks global financial services.

---

## **The Problem: The "Invisible" Workforce**
Over 2 billion people work in the informal economy globally. These workers—artisans, farmers, gig workers—have skills and cash flow, but **no verifiable identity.** Without a formal record, they cannot access credit, insurance, or stable employment. They are "Economically Invisible."

## **The Solution: The Kairo Trust Loop**
Kairo turns everyday work into a verifiable financial reputation. We bridge the gap between the informal streets and the formal banks using:
1.  **AI Verification**: Analyzing "Proof of Work" (photos and voice stories).
2.  **The Trust Score**: A dynamic score that acts as a portable economic passport.
3.  **Squad Integration**: A full-stack banking engine for payments and loans.

---

## **Core Product Modules**
- **AI Hustle Consultant**: A personalized career advisor that uses TTS to brief users on market opportunities and match them with verified employers.
- **Squad-Backed Wallet**: Instant virtual accounts, secure transfers, and micro-loans powered by the Squad API.
- **Economic Heatmap**: Data-driven insights helping workers find high-demand zones in real-time.

---

## **Market Opportunity**
- ** Informal Economy Size**: Accounts for ~50% of GDP in many emerging markets.
- **Target Users**: Millions of skilled artisans and traders in Nigeria looking for formal credit.
- **Scalability**: A universal identity protocol that can be deployed across any emerging market.

---

## **Tech Stack**
- **Frontend**: Expo, React Native, Reanimated.
- **Fintech**: Squad API (Virtual Accounts, Transfers).
- **Security**: BVN Verification, 4-digit Transaction PINs.
- **AI**: Expo Speech, Custom Insight Algorithms.

---

## **The Future**
Kairo is moving beyond just a wallet. We are building the **Universal Trust Protocol**—a world where a carpenter in Lagos can prove his worth to a bank in London with a single tap.
